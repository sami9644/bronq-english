A school built on real conversation

Bronq English School began with a simple idea: people learn a language by using it. From our first classroom in Addis Ababa, we have focused on speaking, listening, and practical writing rather than memorising rules.

Today we teach students at two branches, Megenagna and Summit, preparing them for university, work, travel, and international exams.

Our mission

To give every learner in Ethiopia the English skills to study, work, and connect anywhere in the world, in a classroom where it is safe to make mistakes.
